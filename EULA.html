Content Moderation Process

Reporting

Any user may report content (posts, comments, or profiles). Each user may report a given item once.

Automatic Hiding

When content receives five (5) reports, it is automatically hidden. A moderation is possible after the first (1) report.

Review by Moderators

A Peer Network moderator reviews the reported content against these EULA rules. The moderator may determine:

Hidden Content

Content deemed inappropriate under these rules but not illegal will be marked as hidden.

  • Hidden content is anonymized or labeled accordingly and may still be visible in limited contexts (e.g., on the content owner's profile) but is not shown in the feed.
  • Hidden content can sometimes be accessed with user consent (e.g., by clicking through a disclaimer).
  • Interactions may remain possible unless otherwise restricted.

Illegal Content

Content determined to be illegal under applicable laws will be marked as removed as illegal.

  • Illegal content is removed from the feed, searches, filters, and sharing functions.
  • Interactions (likes, comments, views, rewards) are permanently disabled.
  • Posts flagged as illegal may remain visible in the content owner's profile but will carry a clear label ("Removed as illegal under EULA rules") and cannot be promoted, interacted with, or accessed even with consent.
  • Comments or profiles flagged as illegal will remain visible only in anonymized or restricted form, with no interactions permitted.

Restore Content

If content is reviewed and deemed compliant, it is restored and made visible again. Once restored, the same item cannot be reported again.

User Notifications

Users are not notified of moderation decisions. Users can see:

  • Their ability to report an item once.
  • The current report count of the item.
  • If the content is hidden, and if the content is restored.
