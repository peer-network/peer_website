Daily Actions

In the upper right corner, you'll see small symbols with numbers indicating your free daily actions. These reset daily at 00:00 UTC. Views are unlimited, but likes and comments are limited resources.

How Peer Network Works

Content creators post to attract viewers, who engage with content through likes, comments and views. This engagement generates Gems for creators, which convert to Peer Points.

Earn Gems for interactions

Your content automatically generates Gems when others engage with it.
More engagement equals more earnings.

Daily Interactions

Each user gets a limited number of free daily actions (likes, comments) that reset at 00:00 UTC. Views are unlimited, and you can make one free post per day.

Additional Posts

Want to post more? Each additional post costs 20 Peer Points. Disliking content costs 3 Peer Points per dislike.

Gems for actions

Every 24 hours at 08:00 UTC, your earned Gems of the last day are convert to Peer Points based on your share of the day's total engagement.
