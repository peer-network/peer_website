Content Moderation Process

Reporting

Any user may report content (posts, comments, or profiles). Each user may report a given item once.

Automatic Hiding

When content receives five (5) reports, it is automatically hidden from the platform feed. A moderation review is possible after the first (1) report.

Review by Moderators

A Peer Network moderator reviews the reported content against these EULA rules. The moderator may determine:

  • To Hide Content: Content remains hidden and could be accessible with consent.
  • To Restore Content: Content is restored and visible again.
  • To Remove Content forever: Content is hidden and cannot be accessed.

Restored Content

If the content was restored, it is not possible to report it again.

User Notifications

Users are not notified of moderation decisions. Users can see:

  • Their ability to report an item once,
  • The current report count of the item,
  • If the content is hidden, and if the content is restored.
